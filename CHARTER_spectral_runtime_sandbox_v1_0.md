Establish a **sealed, drift‑neutral isolation environment** for NDH spectral‑runtime execution.

- safe containment for **adjacency**, **resonance**, **topology**, and **integration** flows  
- persistent residency for **RuntimeOrchestrator**  
- altitude‑sealed execution separate from narrative manifolds  
- compatibility with **Shared‑Horizon experimental engines**  
- enforcement of **RP‑ALTITUDE constraints** without expressive‑layer bleed  

This charter defines the membrane, rules, and integration boundaries.
